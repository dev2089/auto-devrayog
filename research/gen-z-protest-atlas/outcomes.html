WINNING THE STREET
IS NOT THE END.

A protest can win a demand and still lose on justice. It can force a resignation without changing institutions. Compare the result type, not one overall score.

01Policy change: a bill, budget, ban or perk is reversed.
02Leadership change: an office-holder leaves.
03Accountability: abuses are investigated through due process.
04Institutional change: rules and services improve and persist.

THE OUTCOME TEST

Who can reverse the win, and what would prevent it?

A durable reform needs documented demands, a responsible institution, enforceable rules, public monitoring and protection for those who check whether promises were delivered.

Measure the whole cycle

Six separate checks.

This is a framework for evaluation, not a ranking of countries or protesters.

Decision

Was the disputed bill, budget or ban reversed?

Leadership

Did officials resign, and was succession lawful?

Justice

Were abuses investigated impartially?

Representation

Did protest leaders gain durable institutional access?

Delivery

Did schools, hospitals, utilities and jobs measurably improve?

Durability

Were changes funded, enforced and protected from reversal?
