Method transparency

How the evidence is weighted.

“Primary” does not always mean “neutral”; “independent” does not mean “infallible.” Match the source to the claim.

Official records

Good for confirming votes and resignations, less suited to independently judging government's own conduct.

Human-rights investigations

Check the witness base, verification process, method and time window. Attribute estimates.

Professional reporting

Useful for chronology; retain attribution for crowd estimates and disputed allegations.

Academic / policy analysis

Useful for mechanisms and comparisons, but interpretation is not direct observation.

Trackers and datasets

Check definitions, inclusion rules, dates, and what each variable can actually measure.
